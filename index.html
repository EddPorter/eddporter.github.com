About

I am a software developer in finance, working mostly on infrastructure tooling and interoperability. I care about getting things right, and about keeping them simple for the people who use them. Where simple is not achievable, I try to be clear about where the complexity sits and why.

Beyond the day job

I enjoy compilers and interpreters, code converters, neural networks, number crunching and operating system design, and the algorithms and data structures underneath them.
